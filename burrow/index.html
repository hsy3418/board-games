How to play

Each player has two animals on a 7×7 garden. On your turn, do two things:

  1. Move one of your animals any distance in a straight line, like a chess queen: across, up/down or diagonal. You can't jump over anything.
  2. Plant a bush 🌿 from where it landed, again in any straight line, on an empty square. Bushes are permanent.

Goal: if it's your turn and none of your animals can move, you lose. So build walls, fence your opponent in, and keep open space for yourself.

Tip: early on, think about territory, not attack. Open space near your animals is your "air".

Based on the Game of the Amazons (Walter Zamkauskas, 1988), shrunk to a 7×7 board.
